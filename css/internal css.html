AWH COLLEGE

AWH College in Kozhikode is a group of institutions run by the Association for the Welfare of the Handicapped (AWH), offering specialized programs in engineering, physiotherapy, genetics, audiology, speech-language pathology, and teacher education. It is recognized for its inclusive approach, modern infrastructure, and affiliation with major universities in Kerala.
